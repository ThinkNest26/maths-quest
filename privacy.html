Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Maths Quest is a maths practice platform for primary schools, provided by ThinkNest. This notice explains what information we keep, why, and how we protect it. We have written it in plain English for schools, teachers and families.

Who is responsible

Each school that uses Maths Quest decides which children and teachers use it and why. The school is responsible for that information (the “controller”). ThinkNest stores and processes it on the school’s behalf and only to provide Maths Quest. Parents with questions about their child’s information should contact their child’s school first; schools can contact us at hello@school-mathquest.com.

What we keep

AboutWhatWhy
ChildrenFirst name, grade, class, a sign-in code, avatar choices, support settings chosen by the teacher, answers, quests, stars, streaks, speed scores, race scores and maths check resultsSo children can sign in, keep their progress, and teachers can see what to teach next
TeachersEmail address, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never visible to us), schoolSo teachers can sign in and manage their classes
SchoolsSchool name, contact name, email, phone and city, plan and datesTo set up the school, manage trials and plans, and send invoices
Interest formSchool, name, role, email and any note you writeTo reply to your enquiry

We do not ask for children’s surnames, dates of birth, photos, email addresses, addresses or phone numbers. Please do not type these into Maths Quest.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of information for marketing.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies, and no social media trackers.
  • No chat between children. Races only show first names and avatars to children in the same school and grade.

Where it is stored

Information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the website is served by GitHub Pages. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Teacher-chosen videos play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and only when a child taps to play them. Some of these providers may store information outside the UAE.

Who can see it

  • Teachers at the child’s school can see that school’s children and progress.
  • Parents and carers can see their own child’s progress using the child’s code. Codes should only be shared with the child’s family.
  • ThinkNest staff can see school and plan details to support schools, and only look at children’s data if a school asks for help.

How long we keep it

We keep a school’s information while the school uses Maths Quest. When a school asks us to close its account, or a plan has ended for more than 12 months, we delete the children’s information. Teachers can delete a child at any time from the teacher page. Race codes are deleted after one day.

Your rights

Schools can ask us to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold for them. Parents should contact the school, which can ask us on their behalf. Email hello@school-mathquest.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Children’s safety

Maths Quest is designed for children aged about 5 to 11 and is used through their school. There are no public profiles, no messaging and no links to outside websites in the children’s area, apart from teacher-chosen videos.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top and tell schools about important changes.
