A journey through maths lands

Each grade has its own quest map that follows the National Curriculum for England. Teachers can change the order of the lands to match their maths plan.

Number IslandCounting, place value, number bonds and mental adding and subtracting.

Fraction FallsHalves, quarters, fractions of amounts, decimals and percentages.

Shape Forest2D and 3D shapes, symmetry, angles, turns and position.
Data DesertTally charts, pictograms, bar charts and tables, with class surveys children can join.

Questions

Which children is it for?

Children from KG2 (Year 1) to Grade 5 (Year 6). Each grade has its own quest map that follows the National Curriculum for England.

Does it help with standardised maths tests?

Yes. Maths Quest builds the skills these tests measure: quick recall, understanding, reasoning and problem solving. It includes listen-and-answer mental maths and practice tests in a similar style. All questions are our own original questions.

Can children use it at home?

Yes. Children sign in with their own quest code on any tablet or computer, at school or at home. No email address is needed for children.

Is it safe for children?

There are no adverts and no chat. Children only use a first name and a secret code, and each school sees only its own children.

We already use the Spelling Bee. Is it connected?

Yes. Maths Quest is part of the same family, so your school account, teacher logins and children's codes will work for both.

How much will it cost?

Prices are per school, per year, with a free trial. Founding schools that register their interest now get a special price.
