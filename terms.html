These terms are between ThinkNest (“we”) and the school that signs up for Maths Quest (“the school”). By starting a trial or a plan, the person signing up confirms they are allowed to agree to these terms for their school.

1. The service

Maths Quest gives children maths practice in seven themed lands, with progress tools for teachers and a read-only view for parents. We may improve and change features over time.

2. Free trial

New schools get a free 7-day trial with everything unlocked. No payment details are needed. At the end of the trial, children cannot play until the school chooses a plan, but nothing is deleted straight away.

3. Plans and payment

Plans are per school, per year. When a school asks for a plan, we send an invoice to the school. Prices are shown on the teacher page when choosing a plan. A plan normally runs to 31 July (the end of the school year) unless we agree otherwise in writing.

4. The school’s responsibilities

  • Only add children and teachers from your own school, and only the information Maths Quest asks for (first name, grade and class).
  • Keep teacher passwords and children’s codes safe, and share a child’s code only with that child and their family.
  • Tell families that the school uses Maths Quest, as you would for any learning platform.
  • Use the platform for teaching and learning, not for anything harmful or unlawful.
